Liandi Franzsen Hockey Coach

Liandi is a born leader with nearly a decade of experience coaching Hockey. A former SWD player, she has a strong focus on the personal development of each of her students — not only teaching core skills, but also sportsmanship and character, helping them grow as individuals and as a team. Liandi believes strong basics, smart thinking and fun are the foundations of lasting success.

Melinda Britz Netball Coach

Melinda is a passionate Sports and Movement Coach with NSA Netball qualifications, alongside many other qualifications in the childcare industry — making her the perfect addition to our Kids Sports Hub. Melinda's goal is to encourage every child to grow in confidence by helping them reach their full potential on and off the court. With a deep love for children, she leads with patience and kindness, helping kids discover the joy of team sports.

Liam Mackrill Performance Lab & Boys Soccer Coach

Liam is an all-round fitness guru, with years of experience as a personal trainer as well as more than 2 years of football coaching experience. He believes 1% improvement every day, compounded over time, is the key to success. Liam always brings the positivity and knows how to get the best out of each and every person he coaches. He leads with kindness and fun, and kids just love him!

Sam February Girls Soccer Coach
