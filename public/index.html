Boys Hockey

From confident stick skills to competitive team play.

About the Programme

Hockey is one of South Africa’s great team sports — fast, skilful and brilliantly suited to young players discovering what they’re capable of. Our programme takes children from their very first grip of the stick through to confident, competitive team play.

Sessions are high-energy and touch-rich: every child spends maximum time on the ball, not standing in queues. As skills grow, so does the game — from small-sided indoor battles to full-field matches where everything comes together.

The 10-Week Structure

Every term is made up of 6 indoor sessions and 4 outdoor sessions, flexed around George’s weather so no class is ever missed. Each track follows its own progression — whatever order the weather allows, your child’s development never skips a step.

Game IntelligenceSessions 5–6
Small-court games, creating and using space, intro to set pieces, attack/defence transitions.

Netball

Footwork, teamwork and game sense — built step by step.

About the Programme

Netball is a game of precision, movement and trust — and one of the most loved team sports in the country. Because every player depends on her teammates to move the ball, netball teaches connection and communication like few other sports can.

Our programme builds the game from the ground up: footwork first, then passing, shooting and dodging, then the positional patterns that turn seven individuals into a team.

Footwork & balancePassing accuracyShooting techniqueDodging & getting freeDefending & markingCourt positioning

Rugby

Fundamentals in a structured, age-appropriate environment.

About the Programme

Rugby builds something special in young people — courage, camaraderie and the knowledge that the team comes first. Our programme introduces the game safely and progressively, with tag and touch formats for younger groups and age-regulated contact progression for older players.

Every session is built around handling, running and game sense first — the skills that make rugby fun — with contact introduced only when players are ready and always within age-group regulations.

Babies & Crawlers

First sounds, first senses, first friends — for baby and for you.

About the Programme

The first eighteen months are a whirlwind of firsts — and this class is a gentle, joyful space to experience them together. Every session is built around you and your baby side by side: floor-based sensory exploration, music, movement and touch, guided by qualified early childhood development practitioners.

There’s no pressure and nothing to perform. Babies engage exactly as much as they want to, naps happen when they happen, and feeding is always welcome. You’ll leave with a calmer baby, a few new ideas to try at home — and other mums who truly get it.
