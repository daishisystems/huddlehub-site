Who We Are

Your People.
Right Here in George.

Cub Hub

For the Early Years.
And the Mums Living Them.

Music & Movement Sensory Play Creative Art Gross Motor Skills Mum & Baby Socials

The early years are extraordinary. They are also exhausting, isolating, and relentless — especially if you are far from family or still finding your feet in a new town.

Cub Hub was created for exactly this season of life. Our term-style 10-week classes create welcoming spaces that nurture genuine connections and real friendships — for little ones and their mums.

Music mornings, creative art play, and gross motor activities help babies and toddlers build confidence, stay active, and make their first friends — while the grown-ups get a proper cup of tea and some conversation worth having.

  • 6 class groups across 3 age bands — two slots per age group
  • Qualified early childhood development practitioners
  • Social time with refreshments after every session
  • Safe, welcoming community hall in George
  • R1,350 per term · 10 sessions
  • Minimum 10 · maximum 20 participants per group — places filled first come, first served

Sports Hub

Their Team.
Their Season to Shine.

Homeschooling and small private schools offer something remarkable — personalised learning, close-knit communities, and the freedom to thrive at your own pace. The one thing they can't always provide is a full team sports programme. That's exactly where HuddleHub steps in.

HuddleHub changes that. Our term-style 10-week programmes create a welcoming sporting environment where children develop real skills, make lasting friendships, and find their team.

Professional coaching in Hockey, Soccer, Netball, Rugby, Fitness and more helps young athletes build confidence, resilience and teamwork — one session at a time.

  • 6 sports codes across 4 age groups (U7 · U9 · U11 · U13)
  • Qualified, vetted coaches with police clearance
  • Competitive team formation and inter-school opportunities
  • R1,350 per term · 10 sessions · 45 minutes each
  • Minimum 10 · maximum 20 participants per group — places filled first come, first served
